    04How it works

    I.

    Suggest

    Search for a film and add it to the pot. You can suggest as many as you like.

    II.

    Fair picks

    Films are picked in rounds. Everyone with a film in the pot gets one pick per round, in random order. Suggesting more films doesn't give you more turns.

    III.

    Content check

    Every film is checked against content notes our members have asked us to avoid. If a film is flagged, it stays out of the pot.

    IV.

    Watch, then talk

    Watch the film in your own time, wherever you like. Then we meet every other Friday lunchtime to discuss it.
